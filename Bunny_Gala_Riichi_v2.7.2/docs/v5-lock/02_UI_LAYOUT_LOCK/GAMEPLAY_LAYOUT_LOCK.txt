BUNNY RIICHI — GAMEPLAY LAYOUT LOCK

CAMERA / HIERARCHY
- Landscape gameplay composition, player looking across a four-sided Riichi table.
- Visual priority: Mahjong tiles > table/game state > profiles/characters > environment.
- Keep discard rivers and hand readable before decorative effects.

SEATING / CHARACTERS
- Wizard = LEFT.
- Ninja = TOP/CENTER opponent.
- King = RIGHT.
- Player = BOTTOM; do NOT show a full-body Yellow Bunny in front of the player's hand.

PLAYER PROFILES
- All four players must have a profile/avatar and score.
- Default starting example score may be 25,000 when illustrating a standard initial state.
- Profiles must not cover tile walls, discard rivers, calls, or player hand.

ROUND / HUD
- Round/game-state information is centralized in the table-center HUD.
- Do NOT duplicate the Round HUD at top-left.
- Top center should remain visually clean; no unnecessary button bar.
- Utility controls may remain top-right: Game Rules / Info / Settings / Exit.

TABLE / CORNERS
- Four decorative corner panels are REMOVED.
- Do not replace them with ornaments merely to fill space.

HAND
- Player hand is large and legible.
- Correct Riichi presentation: 13-tile resting hand; draw state shows 13 + 1 drawn tile with a visible gap before the drawn tile.
- Selected tile may lift upward subtly; do not rely on excessive blue gaming glow.

DISCARD RIVERS
- Show realistic discard rivers to prove readability.
- Organize each player's river in clean rows, normally 6 tiles per row.
- Orient each river toward its owning player consistently.
- Riichi declaration is represented by the appropriate sideways discard tile.

CALLS / MELDS
- Open melds must have reserved readable space near the owning player's side.
- CHI / PON / KAN / RON / TSUMO / PASS are contextual actions, not permanent giant buttons occupying the bottom UI.
- Only show actions that are currently legal/available.

AUTO / TIMER
- Auto-play may be a compact control if the implementation supports it.
- Timer must be clear but secondary to the hand.

TILE BACK
- Every concealed wall tile uses the locked black + gold + ivory design.
- No Bunny Gala logo on tile backs.
